Privacy Policy

Tiny Seal - Last updated: 2026-11-21

1. Overview

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app Tiny Seal (com.nixfelis.tinyseal) by NixFelis ("we"). Tiny Seal is a kid-safe Android launcher / home screen with a parent PIN and app allowlist. We do not collect personal or usage data through Tiny Seal.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Tiny Seal does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or usage data. We do not use accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporters that upload data, or any third-party services that collect data. The app does not phone home.

3. Data on Your Device

The parent PIN and app allowlist are stored only on your device (local preferences). Theme choices and related settings stay on-device as well. This data is never sent to us or to third parties, and is removed when the app is uninstalled (standard Android behavior).

Tiny Seal may use Android’s built-in screen pinning (Lock Task Mode) when enabled in system settings, and may set the device wallpaper via the standard Android wallpaper API. Those features keep images and state on-device; no data leaves the device.

4. Network

Tiny Seal is designed to operate without a network connection for its core launcher and parental-control features. The app does not require an internet connection to run.

5. Children

Tiny Seal is intended for use on a child’s tablet under a parent’s or guardian’s control. Parents configure the PIN and allowlist. The app does not include user-generated content, chat, or social features, and we do not collect data from children.

6. Third-party fonts

Tiny Seal bundles Font Awesome Free under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (font) and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (icon glyphs). License text ships in the APK.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@nixfelis.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of Tiny Seal after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
